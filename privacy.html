HomeHub privacy policy

Effective 2026-10-18

HomeHub is a personal application with a single user, its operator. It accesses Google user data only for the operator's own Google account, with that account's explicit consent through Google OAuth.

Data accessed

Gmail messages, labels, and attachments on the operator's account, used to read, organize, and send mail related to the operator's household records and projects.

Storage

Data is processed and stored only on hardware owned and controlled by the operator. No data is transmitted to third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Limited use

HomeHub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
